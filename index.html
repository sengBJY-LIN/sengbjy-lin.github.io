Research

MmWave sensing & robotics

RFingers

I contribute to a robotic platform that uses multiple 60 GHz radar modules to scan inside drawers from different angles. I study how errors in the robot arm’s end-effector position affect the resulting 3D reconstructions.

AI-assisted fabrication

RF-Fab-DT & MetaClaw

I built a local AI system that generates circuit designs for 3D printing and created a reusable library of generated circuit components.

Radar experimentation

MetaHawk

I designed and tested different covers to compare their effects on radar detectability.
